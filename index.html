I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student at KAIST, in the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI. I am a member of CVLAB, advised by Prof. Seungryong Kim (2025–present). I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University in 2025. Currently, I am a Research Intern at Kakao Corp., where I work with the Visual Content Generative AI team.

My research focuses on visual generation across images and video, along two threads. On the representation side, I study the internal representations of diffusion transformers — how correspondence emerges inside them, and how dense alignment can be used to guide generation. More recently, on the efficiency side, I study how that understanding can guide more efficient generation.
